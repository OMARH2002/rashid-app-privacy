Privacy Policy

Rashid University Attendance App (“Rashid App”)

Developer and publisher: Omar Hany (sole developer and publisher)

1. About this policy

This policy explains what personal information the Rashid University Attendance App (the “App”) collects, why, how it is protected, and the choices and rights you have. The App is developed and published by Omar Hany (“we”, “us”) to manage class attendance for students, lecturers and administrators of Rashid University (the “University”). There is no public registration: accounts are created by authorized App administrators.

2. Who is responsible for your data

Omar Hany, the sole developer and publisher of the App, decides why and how personal information in the App is used and is responsible for it. The University does not operate the App or determine how data in it is processed. Privacy questions can be sent to the contact in Section 14.

3. Information we collect and why

We collect only what the App needs to work. Data is entered by App administrators (account setup), by you (using the App), or automatically (technical data).

4. What we do not collect

Based on the App’s current design, the App does not: track your location; access your contacts, photos, videos, microphone or personal files; use advertising or advertising identifiers; process payments; or sell personal data.

5. Camera permission

The App asks for camera access only so that students can scan the temporary attendance QR code displayed by a lecturer. The camera is used live to read the code. The App does not take, save or upload photos or videos. Only the decoded attendance-session value is sent to the server to record your attendance. You can deny or revoke camera permission in your device settings, but you will not be able to register attendance by QR code.

6. How information is used

We use information to authenticate users; record and display attendance; let lecturers and administrators review attendance records; manage account and device registration; secure the service and investigate misuse; and meet legal obligations. We do not use it for advertising or profiling unrelated to attendance.

7. Sharing and service providers

  • Lecturer and administrator users. Attendance records are visible to authorized lecturers (for their courses) and administrators, according to their role. Students see only their own records.
  • Infrastructure provider. The App’s backend and database are hosted on Cloudflare (Workers and Cloudflare-hosted database). Cloudflare processes data on our behalf to run the service and may generate technical logs under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Other sharing. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising. We may disclose information if required by law or a valid legal process.

8. Security and password protection

Passwords are protected with password hashing and are never stored in plain text. Users may be required to replace a temporary password at first sign-in. Authenticated sessions use tokens, and access to data is restricted by role. No system is completely secure; please keep your credentials private and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

9. Data retention and deletion

Account and attendance records are kept while you are an active student or staff member and for as long as needed to operate the App or meet legal requirements. When data is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymized.

Account deletion. Because accounts are created by administrators rather than through public sign-up, you cannot create or delete one on your own. To request deletion of your account and associated data, use the contact in Section 14. Some records may be kept where we are legally required to retain them.

10. Your rights and how to exercise them

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access the personal information we hold about you, correct inaccurate information (such as your name or program), request deletion, or raise a complaint. Send requests to the contact below with proof of identity. Attendance corrections may require review by the relevant lecturer or administrator.

11. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for university students and staff and is not directed at children. If we learn that information about a child was collected improperly, we will delete it.

12. International transfers and applicable law

Cloudflare operates a global network, so data may be processed in countries other than where you live. This policy is governed by the laws that apply to us. If you are in a region with additional rights (such as the EU/UK), those rights apply to you as required by law.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted at this address.
